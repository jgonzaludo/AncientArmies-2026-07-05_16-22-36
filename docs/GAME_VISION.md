# Ancient Armies — Game Vision

## One-Sentence Pitch

A fast-paced mobile tactical RTS where the player commands formations of individually simulated ancient soldiers and wins through positioning, timing, unit choice, and control of disorder.

## Player Experience

The player should feel that:

* tactical decisions visibly matter
* victories come primarily from good decisions
* battles are fast and stimulating without feeling mindless
* individual soldiers create spectacle and chaos without destroying strategic control
* the battlefield tells the player why they are winning or losing

## Core Design Thesis

The player controls order.

Combat creates disorder.

The player decides when to preserve formation, when to commit more aggressively, when to withdraw, and when to regain control.

The central challenge is balancing:

* predictable formation-level tactics
* individually simulated soldiers
* visually exciting local chaos

The game should avoid both extremes:

* formations that feel like rigid moving spreadsheets
* independent agents whose behavior becomes tactically unreadable

## Combat Philosophy

Combat should be strategically predictable but microscopically variable.

Positioning, unit matchups, morale, terrain, facing, and tactical decisions should eventually determine battle outcomes.

Individual details may vary:

* exactly which soldier attacks first
* which soldier is displaced
* the exact shape of a melee
* individual casualty order

The player should usually understand why an engagement succeeded or failed.

## Core Player Loop

Observe the battlefield.

Position and orient formations.

Choose engagements.

Watch formations collide and develop disorder.

Decide whether to maintain the engagement, break ranks, withdraw, redirect, or reform.

Exploit the result.

## Long-Term Direction

The finished game should include:

* real-time formation control
* melee, ranged, cavalry, and other ancient unit classes
* different formation shapes
* tactically meaningful facing
* morale and routing
* terrain
* enemy strategy and AI
* battles approximately five to ten minutes long
* mobile-first landscape controls
* fixed isometric-style camera with zoom
* stylized chunky low-detail 3D characters

The visual target is readable, playful, and cool rather than realistic or highly detailed.

## V1 Goal

A complete playable battle in which the player controls one army against an autonomous enemy army.
