## Pipeline actually used

1. Candidate A generated by multi-image-to-3D from the three turnaround crops
   (679,214 tris, single fused mesh, one 2048² baked texture).
2. **First cleanup attempt (rejected internally):** material-classified faces,
   separated modules, then Blender collapse-decimated to a 6.9k-tri total.
   Decimating across the open module boundaries tore the shells — visibly
   shredded. Discarded.
3. **Final pipeline:** Meshy **Remesh API** reduced the original fused sculpt
   to 22k tris (5 credits, 61 s), preserving surface quality; then per-face
   texture-color classification (numpy HSV rules) remapped every face to the
   eight shared `MAT_Roman_*` materials; then face-mask separation split the
   mesh into modules along material/position boundaries; **no further
   decimation**.

## Budget deviation (documented)

The 5,000–6,500-tri target was attempted and **failed the visual gate** —
at that density the AI-sculpted armor detail (segmentata bands, cheek guards,
emblem wings) shreds. 22,380 tris is the working compromise pending Unity
mobile profiling; the performance budget is explicitly provisional. Mitigation
options if profiling demands it: Meshy remesh to 12–15k, hidden-face removal
under armor, or LOD1 at distance. At 400–500 soldiers this is ~9–11 M tris
before culling — must be validated in Phase 16 (Unity testing) of the next
session.

## Modules rebuilt from scratch

- `BODY_RomanBase` + `BODY_RomanBase_Blank_Eyes` (blank reusable body):
  native Blender shell construction (skin-modifier core + rounded-box
  head/fists/feet), proportions matched to measurements taken from the Meshy
  skin (head ≈0.58 m wide, arm ≈0.22 m thick, 1.75 m tall, ~2.55 heads),
  fitted-shorts `MAT_Roman_ClothNeutral` region, no equipment.
  The Meshy mesh has no geometry under the armor, so no hidden body was
  pretended into existence (per instructions).

## Known compromises

1. Helmet is one mesh (`HELMET_ImperialBasic_Dome`), not five sub-objects —
   the remeshed dome/cheek/neck/mount share welded geometry; splitting adds
   seams for no gameplay benefit. Future variant helmets swap the whole module.
2. `CLOTH_Belt_Basic` absorbed the baldric strap and back scabbard (contiguous
   leather in the source).
3. Assembled unit's skin is `BODY_LegionarySkin_Basic` (open shells at hidden
   boundaries); the watertight blank `BODY_RomanBase` is a separate reusable
   asset. They are alternates, not layered.
4. Sleeves are part of `CLOTH_Tunic_Basic` (no separate sleeve module) —
   matches source topology.
5. Back skirt shows dense stud/strap detail per the reference back view;
   reads slightly busy at extreme close-up, correct at gameplay distance.
6. Material palette tweaked during cleanup: `MAT_Roman_Skin` →
   (0.95, 0.72, 0.47), `MAT_Roman_ClothRed` → (0.60, 0.11, 0.09).
   **`ROMAN_MATERIAL_PALETTE.md` and the Unity materials must be synced in the
   next (Unity) session** — Unity was intentionally untouched in this task.
